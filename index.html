Our purpose

Make citizen voice easier to understand, prepare and exercise—while keeping people in control of their choices and information.

A clearer path from issue to action

Turn a concern into a considered next step.

Public problems can be difficult to navigate. Janavani’s intended civic-action pathway makes the steps visible, without confusing a draft, a submission attempt and an official acknowledgement.

01

Understand the issue

Describe the problem, record relevant facts and identify what information still needs to be verified.

02

Prepare and review

Organize the facts into a clear draft, check the recipient and requested action, then correct it before use.

03

Act deliberately

Choose how to proceed. Formal submission, receipt confirmation and case tracking require separate, verifiable steps.

Current availability

A useful starting point, with clear limits.

The current online workspace supports local document drafting, preview, download and printing. It is an early product surface, not yet the complete persisted citizen case-management experience.

Authenticated case saving, evidence lifecycle, cross-surface continuity, formal submission, acknowledgements and outcome tracking remain subject to implementation and verification.

Before you continue

Review all generated text for accuracy. Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information. The workspace does not submit documents on your behalf; follow the relevant authority’s official process.

Built around citizen agency

Technology should strengthen participation—not replace judgment.

Privacy by design

Collect and share only what is needed for a clearly understood purpose. Keep optional capabilities under meaningful user control.

Human review

AI-assisted or generated material should be checked by the person using it. Suggestions are not official decisions or legal advice.

Clear evidence

Distinguish citizen-provided information, verified sources, generated content, submissions and official acknowledgements.

Independent access

Web and Telegram are intended to remain independent interfaces over shared capabilities, not competing implementations of the same rules.

The republic begins with the people
