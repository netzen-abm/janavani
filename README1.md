# JANAVANI 🇮🇳
### Rate Any Government Office. Get Instant Legal Complaint Letter.

**Problem**: Citizens go to Panchayat, Hospital, Ration Shop, MVD, Police and face delay, misbehavior, denial of service.

**Solution**: Janavani lets you rate the service and instantly generates a legal complaint letter citing Constitution of India, BSA, IT Act, Consumer Protection Act.

#### Features v1.0
- **Rate + Report**: Hospital, Panchayat, MVD, Traffic Police, Ration Shop, Police Station
- **Legal Brain**: Auto-suggests which law applies to your problem
- **Auto PDF**: Download complaint letter with correct address + CC. Just print and send.
- **100% Anonymous**: No login needed. Powered by Freenet + Nostr.

#### How It Works
1.  Tell Janavani which office + what happened
2.  Rate 1-5 stars
3.  Download legal PDF addressed to the correct authority
4.  Print and send, or email
