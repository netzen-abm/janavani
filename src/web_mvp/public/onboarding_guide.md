# 🏛️ Citizen Onboarding: How to File Official Physical Post Complaints

In the Indian administrative grid, filing petitions via **Physical Registered Post with Acknowledgement Due (RPAD)** or **Speed Post** is a legally recognized way to engage with public bodies. Under municipal codes across South India and North East India, government offices are legally required to accept, log, and respond to these physical filings.

Follow these step-by-step instructions to turn your generated Janavani objection files into formal, real-world civic actions:

---

## 📅 Step 1: Export and Print Your Paperwork
1. Fill out your issue details or browse proposed legislative updates on the **Janavani Web App Dashboard**.
2. Select your preferred format download path:
   * **PDF format** to instantly download a finished document with standard official margins.
   * **Word (.docx) format** if you want to make manual changes or add specific details on your computer first.
3. Print **two physical copies** of the document on clean white A4 paper. 
   * *Why two?* One copy will be mailed to the official recipient, and the second copy will be kept in your personal records as proof of filing.

## ✍️ Step 2: Sign and Date Your Petition
1. Locate the signature line block at the bottom of your printed petition (`Submitted Sincerely, A Concerned Citizen`).
2. Add your handwritten signature and write the current date in blue or black ink on both printed copies.
3. *Note:* Under standard municipal codes, unsigned or completely anonymous physical mailings can be classified as "unsigned communications" and filed away without action.

## ✉️ Step 3: Package the Envelope
1. Buy a standard cloth-lined or durable paper envelope from your local post office.
2. Clearly write the official recipient designation and address (found in your Janavani document block) in the center of the envelope:
   ```text
   TO,
   THE REGIONAL JOINT COMMISSIONER,
   GREATER CHENNAI CORPORATION,
   RIPON BUILDING, EVR SALAI, CHENNAI - 600003
   ```
3. Write your clear delivery return destination information in the bottom-left corner of the envelope envelope layout:
   ```text
   FROM:
   [Your Name / Resident Association Title]
   [Your Residential Address Area Line]
   [City, State, Pincode]
   ```

## 📮 Step 4: Dispatch via India Post Counter
1. Walk into your nearest **India Post Office** branch counter.
2. Request the counter clerk to send the envelope specifically via **Speed Post** or **Registered Post with Acknowledgement Due (RPAD)**.
3. Pay the standard postal fee (typically between ₹20 and ₹40 depending on package weight).
4. **CRITICAL STEP:** Collect the printed payment receipt containing your **13-digit Alphanumeric Tracking Number** (e.g., `EK123456789IN`).

## 🔍 Step 5: Log Your Tracking Data
1. Keep your payment receipt and your personal printed copy of the petition stored safely together.
2. Go to the official [India Post Tracking Portal](https://indiapost.gov.in) to monitor your delivery status.
3. Once the tracking log confirms "Item Delivered", the public body's official review timeline legally begins under standard municipal rules.
