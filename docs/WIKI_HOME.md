## What is Janavani?

Janavani is a full citizen-governance ecosystem. The Dynamic Web/WebApp is the first active product-building surface, but the ecosystem also provides independent Android, iOS, Telegram, Telegram Mini App, WhatsApp, Messenger, API and DApp/Web3 access paths, plus optional local/offline and resilient transport capabilities.

## Core principle

**Capabilities are primary; interfaces are access surfaces.**

Every interface and capability must remain independently operable where practical. No Web, mobile, messaging, DApp, AI/model, transport, storage provider or external service should become an unnecessary single point of failure.

## User choice

Citizens choose which supported surface and optional capability they want to use. AI, Web3, agentic automation, local AI, messaging, offline, mesh and decentralized options must not be silently forced. Material safety, legal, destination, device and network constraints are explicit.

## Constitutional and civic foundation

Janavani operates within India's constitutional and legal environment. The Preamble's **"We, the People of India"** framing is central to the citizen-centered purpose. Articles 14, 19 and 21 are relevant to equality, freedoms, life, personal liberty and privacy-sensitive design where applicable. Article 51A contains citizens' Fundamental Duties and is civic context, not a standalone authorization for Janavani to exercise public authority. The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) is statutory evidence law.

Janavani is not a court, government authority, election authority, law-enforcement body, or substitute for qualified legal representation.

## AI and intelligence families

The ecosystem may use independently replaceable:

- OCR
- Computer Vision
- SAM / segmentation
- VLM
- SLM
- LLM
- MLM
- MoE
- LAM
- RAG
- Agentic AI
- Translation/speech/multimodal services

These are implementation/model families, not mandatory stages of one pipeline. A user-facing capability may use zero, one, or several families.

## Privacy and safety

Privacy by Design, Privacy by Default, Safety by Design and Safety by Default apply across the ecosystem. Data minimization, purpose limitation, consent, identity minimization, provenance, secure evidence handling, user approval, auditability, abuse prevention and truthful delivery states are mandatory design principles.

## Failure isolation

Examples:

- Web down → Telegram/mobile/API continue.
- Telegram down → Web/mobile/API continue.
- LLM down → suitable SLM/deterministic path continues.
- RAG down → truthful degraded/source-unavailable state.
- OCR/CV/VLM/SAM down → manual/non-vision evidence path.
- Agent runtime down → deterministic guided workflow.
- Blockchain down → ordinary civic operation.
- Mesh/satellite down → alternate path or truthful queue.

## Product lifecycle

`Citizen reality → understanding → evidence/context → authority → lawful action → submission → response → tracking → follow-up → outcome → accountability → public learning`
