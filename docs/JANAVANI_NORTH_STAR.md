# JANAVANI — NORTH STAR

**Status:** LOCKED — STRATEGIC NORTH STAR
**Version:** 2.0
**Date:** 23 August 2026

## 1. PURPOSE

Janavani exists to strengthen the citizen's ability to understand public reality, participate in lawful civic action, engage public institutions, obtain responses, pursue follow-up and contribute to accountable governance through privacy-preserving technology.

Janavani is being built as a **full citizen-governance ecosystem**. It is not defined by an MVP, a Telegram bot, a website, an AI model, a PDF generator, a database, or a particular decentralized protocol.

## 2. NORTH STAR QUESTION

> **Can Janavani reduce the distance between a citizen's lived reality and informed, lawful, effective, accountable government action?**

## 3. THE CITIZEN-GOVERNMENT LOOP

```text
Citizen Reality
      ↓
Understanding
      ↓
Evidence / Context
      ↓
Correct Authority
      ↓
Lawful Citizen Action
      ↓
Submission / Communication
      ↓
Government Response
      ↓
Tracking
      ↓
Follow-up / RTI / Escalation
      ↓
Outcome
      ↓
Accountability
      ↓
Public Learning
      ↓
Better Governance
```

Janavani should progressively strengthen every part of this loop.

## 4. ECOSYSTEM IDENTITY

Janavani is one platform experienced through multiple independent surfaces:

- Dynamic Web
- Android
- iOS
- Telegram Bot
- Telegram Mini App
- WhatsApp
- Messenger
- API
- DApp / Web3 interfaces
- Decentralized/resilient transport where justified

Interfaces are mechanisms for access. Citizen capability is the enduring product.

## 5. WHAT JANAVANI ENABLES

The ecosystem may progressively help citizens:

- Understand public problems
- Identify relevant authorities
- Access verified government information
- Create complaints, grievances, RTIs, representations, petitions, appeals and other lawful documents
- Organize and submit evidence
- Submit or communicate through appropriate channels
- Track responses
- Generate follow-ups and escalations
- Discover government schemes and services
- Understand laws, bills, rules and notifications
- Correct public information
- Participate in service feedback
- Explore accountable public information
- Connect with verified experts, NGOs and institutions
- Use emergency/SOS capabilities where available

## 6. GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE

The long-term ecosystem may connect public information across:

```text
Manifesto / Commitment
        ↓
Policy / Programme
        ↓
Budget
        ↓
Allocation / Expenditure
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Service / Project Delivery
        ↓
Citizen Experience
        ↓
Outcome
```

Janavani should prioritize authoritative sources and preserve provenance. Citizen reports, analysis and AI assistance must not be silently presented as verified facts.

## 7. ACCOUNTABILITY

Accountability includes both identifying failures and recognizing effective public service.

Potential domains include offices, services, officers, departments, representatives, schemes, projects and government performance.

Public accountability features require evidence, transparent methodology, privacy, anti-abuse controls, fairness and right-of-response safeguards.

## 8. CONSTITUTIONAL AND LEGAL ORIENTATION

Janavani supports lawful civic participation. It may explain constitutional principles, statutes, rules, procedures and public information, but it is not a court, government authority, or substitute for qualified legal representation.

The system must distinguish authoritative legal material from explanation, inference and AI assistance.

## 9. PRIVACY / CITIZEN CONTROL

The North Star includes citizen control over personal information.

Core principles are:

- Privacy by Design
- Privacy by Default
- Minimum necessary collection
- Consent
- Identity minimization
- Transparent provenance
- User review and approval
- Secure evidence handling

## 10. RESILIENCE

Janavani may progressively support Internet, local, mesh, gateway and satellite-capable transport for appropriate capabilities, particularly emergency and resilience use cases.

Resilience must never be confused with mandatory use of a specific protocol.

## 11. AI

AI should reduce bureaucratic burden without creating new uncertainty.

AI is a replaceable capability for issue understanding, classification, drafting, language, retrieval, document assistance and other controlled functions.

AI must remain bounded by provenance, privacy, verification and human approval requirements.

## 12. FINAL NORTH STAR

**Citizen reality → informed understanding → lawful action → government response → follow-up → accountability → public learning.**

Janavani's purpose is to make that loop more accessible, more transparent, more evidence-based, more privacy-preserving and more resilient for citizens.
