# Janavani Product Maturity

## Canonical classification

Janavani is no longer treated as an MVP-only product. The architecture has evolved into a **full-fledged civic technology ecosystem/platform** with multiple independent interfaces, shared capability infrastructure, policy enforcement, optional AI services, and future decentralized components.

### Current product framing

**Janavani — The Infrastructure of Citizen Voice**

Civic infrastructure for meaningful citizen participation.

## Ecosystem surfaces

The platform is designed to support independent surfaces including:

- Web application
- Android
- iOS
- Telegram Bot / Mini App
- WhatsApp
- Messenger
- AI services
- Agentic AI services
- Future DApp / decentralized components

A failure of one interface must not become a systemic failure of the ecosystem.

## Architecture principle

Interfaces consume shared capability contracts and policy infrastructure; they do not define authority independently.

```text
Interfaces
    ↓
Identity / Session
    ↓
Capability Contracts
    ↓
Authorization + Consent + Safety Policy
    ↓
Shared Services
    ↓
Evidence / Documents / Civic Actions
```

## AI principle

AI remains optional and user-controlled. Core civic capabilities must not depend on AI availability.

## Product-development implication

Repository work, security reviews, testing, deployment planning, and architecture documents should refer to Janavani as an **ecosystem/platform**, not as an MVP, unless a specific historical MVP artifact is being discussed.

Historical MVP references may remain in archived documentation for provenance and project history.
