# JanaVani RTI Strategy

## Purpose

JanaVani must not assume that every civic problem should begin with a complaint.
When a citizen narrates a problem, the shared civic-action planner should first
identify the underlying objective:

- obtain a service/remedy;
- obtain government-held information;
- obtain both a remedy and information;
- preserve evidence/records;
- follow up or escalate an existing matter.

The resulting plan may recommend:

1. RTI first;
2. complaint first, then RTI;
3. complaint and RTI together;
4. complaint first, followed by RTI after a defined procedural/event trigger;
5. follow-up/escalation without RTI;
6. evidence-preservation support before another step.

These are recommendations, not mandatory sequences. The plan must be checked
against current verified law, jurisdiction, authority, and procedure before a
citizen-facing document is prepared.

## RTI design principles

An RTI request is an information-seeking action. It should be distinguished from
a request asking an authority to perform a service or remedy a grievance. A
single case may legitimately need both tracks.

JanaVani should help the citizen turn a narrative into precise, record-oriented
questions and identify the appropriate public authority/PIO using verified
sources. It should also preserve continuity between the original case, RTI,
responses, and applicable appeals.

## Source guide: System Out Complete

JanaVani's RTI design may use the practical ideas publicly described for
**System Out Complete** by **Prasanth Nair IAS (Malayalam, Manorama Books)** as a
conceptual guide, with attribution. Public publisher material describes the
book as covering how to prepare RTI applications, formulate questions, handle
late/refused/unavailable information, and pursue complaint/appeal routes. It
also emphasizes moving from general anger to precise questions and documentary
evidence.

This is a design influence, not a substitute for the Right to Information Act,
current rules, official procedures, or legal advice. JanaVani must not reproduce
copyrighted text from the book or present its contents as official law.

Reference: Prasanth Nair IAS, *System Out Complete*, Manorama Books, Malayalam.
Publisher/book information: https://subscribe.manoramaonline.com/product/system-out-complete

## Privacy

RTI drafting must minimize personal information. Raw sensitive evidence remains
on the user's device by default. AI receives only the minimum sanitized context
needed for the selected capability and only when the user chooses AI.

## Submission boundary

JanaVani prepares and delivers the final RTI document as PDF or editable DOCX.
The citizen decides what happens after delivery. JanaVani does not file the RTI,
send it by email, post it, or otherwise submit it.
