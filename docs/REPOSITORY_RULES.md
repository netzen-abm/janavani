# Repository Rules

## 1

One responsibility per folder.

## 2

No duplicate implementations.

## 3

No business logic inside adapters.

## 4

Domain must not depend on Telegram.

## 5

Workflow must not depend on storage.

## 6

Services coordinate business logic.

## 7

Storage owns persistence.

## 8

Documents own document generation.

## 9

Privacy before convenience.

## 10

Security is part of every design decision.
